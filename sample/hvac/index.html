    HOW THIS IS CALCULATED

    The numbers, explained.

    Citation score: 100 × weighted checks citing your domain ÷ weighted completed answer checks. Service questions have weight 2; business-name questions have weight 1. Failures and missing AI Overviews are excluded. This is a single-scan score.

    Citation rate: unweighted share of completed answer checks with your domain cited. “First mention” means among the first three cited sources; it is not a search ranking.

    Search demand: estimated monthly traditional Google search volume, with its geographic source shown per query. The total is a sum of query estimates; audiences may overlap. It does not measure AI search demand or missed customers.

    Coverage: Google AI Overviews via DataForSEO and Perplexity’s Agent API. API answers can differ from consumer answers. Gemini and consumer ChatGPT are not covered.

    Reports can be viewed by anyone with their link. No email addresses appear in reports.
