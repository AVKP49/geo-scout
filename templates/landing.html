A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you scan.

What does the citation score mean?

The percentage of completed checks that cited your website, weighted toward service questions. Failed checks and missing Google AI Overviews are excluded and shown separately. Every report includes the calculation.

Will this tell me how many customers I’m missing?

No. We can show estimated traditional search demand and sampled citations. These do not establish lost customers, lost revenue or actual AI search volume.

Does a recommendation change my website?

You review and apply any change yourself. The report gives you an evidence-based starting point. A suggested action is not proof of why an engine omitted your business.

Can I share the report?

Yes. Anyone with the report link can view it. The report does not include your email, and we ask search engines not to index it.
