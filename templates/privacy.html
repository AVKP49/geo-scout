PRIVACY & DATA

A clear view of your data.

What a scan sends

Your business website, city and chosen questions are used to check your public website and query DataForSEO and Perplexity. Those providers process submitted information under their own terms. Do not put private information in your questions.

What we store

Business details, your chosen questions, limited public website excerpts, citation evidence, costs and scan events. IP addresses are hashed for abuse prevention. API credentials do not appear in report links or application logs.

Sharing

Anyone with a report link can view that report. Reports are excluded from search indexing instructions, but a recipient can still share a link. Email addresses are stored separately and never displayed in reports. Sample reports are fictional and never mixed with scan records.

Email

We only send report emails after you request them. Postmark processes the delivery. Every follow-up includes an unsubscribe link. We do not sell addresses or use purchased lists.

Retention and requests

Free scans and captured emails are retained for 90 days, then deleted using the maintenance command. You can request deletion by replying to a report email. Requests are processed within 30 days. The site is currently a preview; email and live scans are unavailable until services are connected.
